# 📊 Sales Performance & Revenue Insights Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive **Sales Performance Dashboard** developed in **Microsoft Power BI** to analyze sales performance across products, categories, cities, customers, and time.

The dashboard was designed to transform raw sales data into actionable business insights that can support decision-making around revenue performance, product performance, customer activity, and geographical sales.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

The primary objectives of this analysis were to:

- Monitor overall sales performance and revenue trends.
- Compare current-year sales with previous-year performance.
- Identify the highest-performing products.
- Analyze revenue contribution by product category.
- Identify cities generating the highest sales.
- Monitor sales quantity and quantity growth.
- Understand customer and order performance.
- Provide management with an interactive view of key sales KPIs.

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Microsoft Power BI**
- **Power Query** – Data cleaning and transformation

## 🔍 Key Insights

### Revenue by Product

**Laptop Pro** was the highest-performing product, generating approximately **₦120.5M** and contributing **23.25%** of total sales.

**Keyboard 200** followed with approximately **₦118.5M**, contributing **22.87%**.

Together, these two products represented a significant share of overall sales.

### Revenue by Category

The **Mobile category** generated approximately **₦642.2M**, making it the largest revenue-generating category and contributing **41.14%** of total sales.

The **Electronics category** followed with approximately **₦543.7M**, contributing **34.84%**.

### Revenue by City

**Benin City** generated approximately **₦233.47M**, representing **22.18%** of total sales.

**Ibadan** followed with approximately **₦211.26M**, contributing **20.07%**.

### Sales Growth

The dashboard recorded **8.23% quantity growth**, providing an indication of increased sales volume compared with the previous period.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, businesses can:

1. Closely monitor high-performing products such as Laptop Pro and Keyboard 200.
2. Maintain adequate inventory for products and categories generating the highest revenue.
3. Investigate opportunities to improve performance in lower-performing categories.
4. Analyze customer behavior across different cities and demographic segments.
5. Use monthly sales trends to improve sales forecasting and planning.
6. Continuously monitor current-year performance against previous-year results.
